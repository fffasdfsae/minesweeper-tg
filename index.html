How to Play

Reveal Cells

Tap on a cell to reveal what's underneath. If it's empty, adjacent cells will automatically be revealed too.

Numbers

Numbers indicate how many mines are adjacent to that cell. Use these clues to figure out where the mines are hidden.

Flagging

Long press on a cell to place a flag where you think a mine is. Long press again to remove the flag.

Winning

Clear all non-mine cells to win the game. You don't need to flag every mine — just reveal all safe cells.

Easy Digging

When a revealed number has all its mines flagged around it, tapping it will automatically reveal the remaining adjacent cells.

Easy Flagging

When a revealed number has exactly as many unrevealed neighbors as its value, tapping it will automatically flag all those neighbors.
